## Synthesis & Key PM Findings

1. **The 'Visual Pressure' Hypothesis Validated:**
   - 100% of interviewed applicants stated that seeing the officer's face and reactive expressions created real conversational urgency compared to text prompts.
2. **Optimal Session Duration:**
   - 90 seconds (5 questions) is the sweet spot. Users are willing to do 2–4 repeat sessions in one sitting without fatigue.
3. **Primary Willingness to Pay Driver:**
   - The line-by-line 214(b) risk breakdown and specific answer rewrites are the highest perceived-value features.
